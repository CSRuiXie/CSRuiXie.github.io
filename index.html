About

About

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Ying Tai. Before that, I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science from Southwest University, advised by Prof. Jun Zhou.

I build visual generation and understanding systems, currently with internship experience in Tencent HunyuanVideo foundation model post-training.

My work focuses on video generation, reinforcement learning, and multimodal models, with publications in ICCV, ICLR, CVPR, and PR.
